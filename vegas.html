Bonus stop · One night in Las Vegas

Las Vegas

One evening, done right: the fountains, a Strip walk, downtown's light show, and either a big-ticket show or the free spectacle of the casino floors. Everything below is in evening order.

One night — hour by hour

A single-evening rhythm through the best of Vegas, timed to when each thing is at its best.

  • Evening

    Bellagio Fountains & Conservatory

    Start at the Fountains — they run every 30 minutes by day and every 15 minutes from 8pm to midnight, and they're best after dark. Step inside for the free Conservatory & Botanical Gardens; 15 minutes is plenty.

  • Evening

    Walk the Strip (south stretch)

    Bellagio to Caesars to the Venetian is the most walkable mile: street performers, the Forum Shops, and the Venetian's Grand Canal Shoppes are all free to wander. Stay on the east side crossings — jaywalking tickets are real.

  • Dinner

    Eat on the Strip

    Cheap and fast: In-N-Out at 3545 Las Vegas Blvd or Tacos El Gordo. One proper splurge: the classic Vegas steakhouse — Golden Steer (off-Strip, old-school Rat Pack room) or any of the big-name rooms inside the resorts.

  • Night

    Fremont Street Experience

    Head downtown for the LED canopy light show — free, every hour on the hour after dark. SlotZilla zipline flies you right under the canopy if you want the ride version, and the vintage casinos (Golden Nugget, El Cortez) are steps away.

  • Night

    High Roller or the Sphere

    The LINQ's High Roller is a slow 30-minute ride, best after dark when the whole Strip is lit. Or skip the ticket: stand outside the Sphere and watch the Exosphere — the giant LED skin runs free shows all evening.

  • Late night

    A show, or the casino floor

    One show is the classic Vegas night: Cirque du Soleil's O (Bellagio), KÀ (MGM Grand), or Michael Jackson ONE (Mandalay Bay) — book ahead, same-day tickets are scarce. Otherwise just wander a casino floor: people-watching is free and the spectacle is the point.
