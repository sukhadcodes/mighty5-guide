Stop 1 · The Narrows & red canyon walls

Zion National Park

Steep sandstone walls rise straight out of the Virgin River. Most trails start from the Zion Canyon shuttle and climb from a cool canyon floor to hot, exposed rim views.

Best overall time to visit: Late September–October, or April–May
  • 01

    Angels Landing

    The park's signature hike: steep switchbacks (Walter's Wiggles) then a final chain-assisted ridge scramble with sheer drop-offs on both sides.

    Distance
    4.4–5.4 mi RT
    Elevation
    1,500 ft gain
    Difficulty
    Strenuous
    Time needed
    3–4 hrs

    Best time: Spring & fall mornings — a permit (lottery) is required, and afternoons get hot and crowded.

  • 02

    The Narrows (bottom-up)

    Wade the Virgin River between thousand-foot slot canyon walls. Most people hike the first few miles from Riverside Walk to Big Springs and turn back.

    Distance
    Up to 9.4 mi RT
    Elevation
    Minimal
    Difficulty
    Moderate
    Time needed
    2–8 hrs (flexible)

    Best time: May, June & September for water flow; closed after heavy rain due to flash-flood risk.

  • 03

    Emerald Pools

    A family favorite past waterfalls and a red-rock amphitheater. Lower Pool is flat and paved; Upper Pool adds a rockier climb.

    Distance
    0.6–3 mi RT
    Elevation
    Up to 350 ft gain
    Difficulty
    Easy
    Time needed
    1–3 hrs

    Best time: Spring for the fullest waterfalls; year-round otherwise.

  • 04

    Riverside Walk

    A shaded, mostly flat paved path along the Virgin River — also the approach trail to The Narrows.

    Distance
    2.2 mi RT
    Elevation
    Minimal
    Difficulty
    Easy
    Time needed
    ~1 hr

    Best time: Year-round; pleasant even at midday in summer thanks to the canyon shade.

  • 05

    Watchman Trail

    Steady switchbacks near the south entrance climbing to sweeping views over Springdale and lower Zion Canyon — a quieter alternative to the shuttle-stop hikes.

    Distance
    3.3 mi RT
    Elevation
    ~600 ft gain
    Difficulty
    Moderate
    Time needed
    ~2 hrs

    Best time: Late afternoon for sunset light; fall and spring for comfortable temperatures.

  • 06

    Canyon Overlook Trail

    A short, rocky trail from the Zion–Mt. Carmel Tunnel out to a viewpoint over lower Zion Canyon — big payoff for little effort.

    Distance
    1 mi RT
    Elevation
    160 ft gain
    Difficulty
    Easy
    Time needed
    1–2 hrs

    Best time: Sunset, year-round; the small trailhead lot fills fast.
