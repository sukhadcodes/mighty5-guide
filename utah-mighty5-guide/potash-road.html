Bonus stop · Scenic byway along the Colorado River

Potash Road (UT-279)

A paved 17-mile detour off US-191 just north of Arches, running downriver beneath sheer cliffs — petroglyphs, dinosaur tracks, and three arches with almost none of the park crowds.

Best overall time to visit: Year-round; avoid right after rain — the dirt continuation floods and the Corona Arch trail gets greasy
  • 01

    Corona Arch & Bowtie Arch Trail

    The one hike worth planning around on this road — a moderate slickrock trail with cable-assisted sections leading to two dramatic arches close together.

    Distance
    3 mi RT
    Elevation
    ~480 ft gain
    Difficulty
    Moderate
    Time needed
    2–3 hrs

    Best time: Morning starts, spring and fall; there's no shade on the approach.

  • 02

    Wall Street Petroglyph Panel

    Roadside Native American rock art panel around mile 5.5, visible right from the pullout beneath a popular rock-climbing wall.

    Distance
    Roadside
    Elevation
    None
    Difficulty
    Easy
    Time needed
    10–15 min

    Best time: Anytime; look up at the cliff face just after parking.

  • 03

    Dinosaur Tracks

    A short, sometimes-sketchy scramble up a sandstone slope reveals real 150-million-year-old three-toed theropod footprints, plus a second petroglyph panel higher up.

    Distance
    ~100 yards up-slope
    Elevation
    Short scramble
    Difficulty
    Moderate
    Time needed
    15–20 min

    Best time: Dry conditions only — the scramble gets slick and unsafe when wet.

  • 04

    Jug Handle Arch

    A tall, narrow arch visible right from the road around mile 13.5, with a short walk back to its base and a few more petroglyphs nearby.

    Distance
    Roadside pullout
    Elevation
    Minimal
    Difficulty
    Easy
    Time needed
    10 min

    Best time: Anytime; easy to combine with the drive itself, no dedicated trip needed.

  • 05

    Colorado River & Potash Ponds Overlooks

    Several pull-outs along the river with views of the turquoise potash evaporation ponds and the sheer cliffs the road runs beneath.

    Distance
    Roadside
    Elevation
    None
    Difficulty
    Easy
    Time needed
    Flexible

    Best time: Late afternoon light on the cliffs; good rest stops between hikes.

  • 06

    Musselman Arch (bonus, 4x4 needed)

    Where the pavement ends and the road becomes the Shafer Trail toward Canyonlands — a flat sandstone arch you can walk across, but only reachable with a high-clearance 4WD vehicle.

    Distance
    Short walk once you arrive
    Elevation
    Minimal
    Difficulty
    Strenuous
    Time needed
    Add 1–2 hrs driving

    Best time: Only attempt in a suitable vehicle and dry conditions; check road status before heading out.
