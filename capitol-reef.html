Capitol Reef National Park

The least crowded of the five — a 100-mile wrinkle in the earth's crust, with a fruit-tree oasis at Fruita and slickrock canyons just off Highway 24.

Best overall time to visit: March–May or September–October
  • 01

    Hickman Bridge

    A short, well-loved climb through a side canyon ending at a 133-foot natural stone bridge framing Capitol Dome in the distance.

    Distance
    1.8 mi RT
    Elevation
    400 ft gain
    Difficulty
    Moderate
    Time needed
    1–1.5 hrs

    Best time: Early morning — the small trailhead lot on Hwy 24 fills by 9am in spring and fall.

  • 02

    Cassidy Arch

    The park's flagship hike: a steep climb up slickrock from Grand Wash to the rim above a 130-foot arch you can walk right up to the edge of.

    Distance
    3.4 mi RT
    Elevation
    670 ft gain
    Difficulty
    Strenuous
    Time needed
    2–3 hrs

    Best time: Spring and fall; hike early and avoid summer afternoon thunderstorms.

  • 03

    Capitol Gorge (to the Tanks)

    A flat walk through a narrow, sheer-walled wash past the pioneer 'Register' inscriptions and natural water-catching tanks in the rock.

    Distance
    2.2 mi RT
    Elevation
    ~100 ft gain
    Difficulty
    Easy
    Time needed
    1–1.5 hrs

    Best time: Anytime it hasn't rained — skip this one if storms are forecast anywhere upstream.

  • 04

    Grand Wash

    A wide, flat wash walk between narrowing canyon walls that rival Zion's Narrows in scale, with an easy turnaround point at any distance.

    Distance
    Up to 4.4 mi RT
    Elevation
    ~50 ft gain
    Difficulty
    Easy
    Time needed
    1.5–2.5 hrs

    Best time: Good family option most of the year; avoid after or during rain.

  • 05

    Cohab Canyon Trail

    A steeper side-canyon climb above the Fruita campground to a ridge with views over the orchards and the Fremont River.

    Distance
    3.4 mi RT
    Elevation
    ~400 ft gain
    Difficulty
    Moderate
    Time needed
    1.5–2 hrs

    Best time: Cooler mornings, spring or fall.

  • 06

    Goosenecks Overlook & Sunset Point

    Two short roadside walks to canyon-rim viewpoints, best paired together for one of the easiest sunsets in the whole park.

    Distance
    Under 0.5 mi RT combined
    Elevation
    Minimal
    Difficulty
    Easy
    Time needed
    20–30 min

    Best time: Golden hour, any season.
