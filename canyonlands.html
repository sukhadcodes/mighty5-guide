Stop 4 · Island in the Sky

Canyonlands National Park

A mesa-top plateau over 1,000 feet above the Colorado and Green Rivers. Short trails lead to some of Utah's biggest views for very little walking.

Best overall time to visit: March–May or September–October

Time of day — where to be, when

A rough one-day rhythm through the park's best spots, timed to light and crowds rather than just a popularity order.

  • Sunrise

    Mesa Arch

    Arrive well before first light — this is the single most photographed sunrise spot in the park, and the small viewpoint fills up.

  • Morning

    White Rim Overlook Trail

    A quieter rim view than Mesa Arch, best enjoyed while the morning air is still cool.

  • Midday

    Upheaval Dome or Green River Overlook

    Both have a breeze and a bit of shade at the picnic areas — good rest stops when the sun is harsh.

  • Sunset

    Grand View Point

    The park's best sunset overlook, with light raking across the White Rim and canyons below.

  • 01

    Mesa Arch

    A short loop to a small arch perched right on the canyon rim — the single most photographed sunrise spot in the park.

    Distance
    0.6–0.7 mi loop
    Elevation
    ~90 ft gain
    Difficulty
    Easy
    Time needed
    30–45 min

    Best time: Sunrise, especially fall and spring; arrive well before first light for a spot at the arch.

  • 02

    Grand View Point Trail

    A flat rim walk at the end of the scenic drive with sweeping panoramas over the White Rim and the canyons beyond — the park's best sunset overlook.

    Distance
    1.8–2 mi RT
    Elevation
    ~150 ft gain
    Difficulty
    Easy
    Time needed
    ~1 hr

    Best time: Late afternoon into sunset; fully exposed, so bring sun protection.

  • 03

    White Rim Overlook Trail

    A quieter alternative to Grand View Point with a different angle on the White Rim sandstone bench far below.

    Distance
    1.8 mi RT
    Elevation
    ~170 ft gain
    Difficulty
    Easy
    Time needed
    ~1 hr

    Best time: Late morning; noticeably less crowded than Mesa Arch or Grand View Point.

  • 04

    Upheaval Dome (Crater View Trail)

    A short, steeper climb to viewpoints over a mysterious two-mile-wide crater — likely either a meteorite impact or a collapsed salt dome.

    Distance
    1.6 mi RT to 2nd overlook
    Elevation
    ~370 ft gain
    Difficulty
    Moderate
    Time needed
    1–1.5 hrs

    Best time: Cooler parts of the day; there's little shade on the exposed rock.

  • 05

    Green River Overlook

    A short, nearly flat walk from the picnic area to a wide, breezy overlook of the Green River canyon — a good midday stop when light is harsh elsewhere.

    Distance
    0.5 mi RT
    Elevation
    Minimal
    Difficulty
    Easy
    Time needed
    20 min

    Best time: Midday, when the overlook's breeze and shade make it a good rest stop.

  • 06

    Shafer Canyon & Buck Canyon Overlooks

    Two quick roadside pull-outs near the park entrance with big views straight down into the switchbacks of the Shafer Trail.

    Distance
    Roadside, under 0.2 mi each
    Elevation
    Minimal
    Difficulty
    Easy
    Time needed
    10–15 min each

    Best time: Anytime — good first and last stops on the scenic drive.
