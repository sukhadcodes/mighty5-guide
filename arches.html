Stop 5 · The world's largest arch density

Arches National Park

Over 2,000 documented stone arches packed into a small park just north of Moab. Book a timed-entry reservation for the busy spring–fall season.

Best overall time to visit: April–May or September–October

Time of day — where to be, when

A rough one-day rhythm through the park's best spots, timed to light and crowds rather than just a popularity order.

  • Sunrise

    Delicate Arch

    Cooler temperatures and far fewer people than the midday crowds make sunrise the best time for the exposed, uphill approach.

  • Morning

    Devils Garden Trail

    Little shade along this trail, so mornings are far more comfortable than afternoon heat.

  • Midday

    Sand Dune Arch

    Tucked in a narrow, shaded sandy passage — a good cool break for kids in the heat of the day.

  • Sunset

    Windows Loop & Double Arch

    One of the most rewarding sunset spots in the park, and everything sits within a short walk of one lot.

  • 01

    Delicate Arch

    Utah's most famous silhouette — the one on the license plate — reached via an exposed, mostly uphill slickrock trail with no shade.

    Distance
    3–3.4 mi RT
    Elevation
    480 ft gain
    Difficulty
    Moderate
    Time needed
    2–3 hrs

    Best time: Sunrise or sunset; the exposed final approach is brutal in summer midday sun.

  • 02

    Devils Garden Trail

    The park's best full hike, passing Landscape Arch — the longest arch in North America — before the Primitive Loop scrambles across sandstone fins to Double O Arch and beyond.

    Distance
    1.6–7.8 mi RT depending on turnaround
    Elevation
    Up to 1,350 ft gain (full loop)
    Difficulty
    Strenuous
    Time needed
    1–4 hrs

    Best time: Early morning or late afternoon; the trail is fully exposed with little shade.

  • 03

    Windows Loop & Double Arch

    A cluster of huge, easy-to-reach arches — North & South Window, Turret Arch, and the massive twin-legged Double Arch — all within a short walk of one parking area.

    Distance
    ~1.6 mi RT combined
    Elevation
    Minimal
    Difficulty
    Easy
    Time needed
    45–75 min

    Best time: Sunset is especially good here; one of the most crowded areas midday.

  • 04

    Park Avenue

    A one-way walk beneath towering sandstone fins that resemble a skyline of skyscrapers — best done as a shuttle hike if you can arrange a second car.

    Distance
    2 mi one-way (park to park)
    Elevation
    ~320 ft change
    Difficulty
    Moderate
    Time needed
    1–1.5 hrs

    Best time: Morning light on the fins; can be walked either direction.

  • 05

    Broken Arch & Sand Dune Arch

    Two lesser-known arches near the Devils Garden campground — Sand Dune Arch tucked in a narrow, sandy fin passage, Broken Arch reached via an open loop.

    Distance
    0.4–2 mi RT combined
    Elevation
    Minimal
    Difficulty
    Easy
    Time needed
    30–60 min

    Best time: Anytime; a good shaded midday break for kids in the sandy slot near Sand Dune Arch.
